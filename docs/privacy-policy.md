# Cap 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

- 生效日期：2026-11-12
- 适用产品：Cap（macOS，GitHub 分发版与 Mac App Store 版）

## 中文

Cap 是一款在本机运行的 macOS 截图工具。

1. **截图与识别**：截图、标注、滚动拼接、OCR 全部在你的 Mac 上完成，不会上传到开发者服务器。
2. **本地数据**：截图历史保存在本机（App Store 版在应用沙盒容器内；GitHub 版在 `~/Library/Application Support/Cap/`）。你可以在 App 内随时清空历史。
3. **保存位置**：截图默认保存到 `~/Pictures/Screenshots/`，也可以选择其他目录。
4. **权限**：屏幕录制权限用于截屏；辅助功能权限仅用于「滚动长截图」自动滚动目标窗口，可在系统设置中随时关闭。
5. **上传（可选）**：只有你在设置里填写上传端点并主动上传时，图片才会通过 HTTP(S) POST 到**你自己配置的服务器**；开发者不参与该传输、不接收、不存储这些数据。请自行确认目标服务器的隐私政策。
6. **无跟踪**：不包含分析 SDK、不收集使用数据、不投放广告、不创建账号。
7. **变更**：如政策有变，会更新本页面并标注日期。

## English

Cap is a macOS screenshot tool that runs entirely on your Mac.

1. **Capture & recognition**: captures, annotations, scrolling stitch, and OCR all happen on-device and are never uploaded to the developer.
2. **Local data**: screenshot history is stored locally (App Store build: inside the app sandbox container; GitHub build: `~/Library/Application Support/Cap/`). You can clear it from the app at any time.
3. **Saved files**: screenshots are saved to `~/Pictures/Screenshots/` by default, or a folder you choose.
4. **Permissions**: Screen Recording is used to capture the screen. Accessibility is optional and only used by Scrolling Capture to scroll the target window automatically; you can revoke it in System Settings.
5. **Upload (optional)**: only when you configure an upload endpoint and explicitly upload, the image is POSTed over HTTP(S) to **a server you control**. The developer is not involved in, does not receive, and does not store that data. Please review your server's privacy practices.
6. **No tracking**: no analytics SDKs, no usage data collection, no ads, no accounts.
7. **Changes**: updates will be published on this page with a new effective date.
